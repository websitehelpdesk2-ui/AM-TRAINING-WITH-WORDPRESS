Programs Offered

At AM Training Institute, we offer two comprehensive programs designed to prepare you for a rewarding career in the healthcare field. Our programs combine online learning with hands-on clinical training to ensure you gain the skills and knowledge needed to succeed.

Dental Assistant

Our Dental Assistant Program equips students with the essential skills needed to assist in dental procedures and patient care. Graduates can work in dental offices, clinics, and hospitals.
Learn more

CNA

The CNA Program prepares students to provide essential care to patients in various healthcare settings. CNA graduates can find employment in nursing homes, hospitals, and home health agencies.
