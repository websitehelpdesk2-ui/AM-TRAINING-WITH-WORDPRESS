Introduction to FAQs

At AM Training Institute, we understand that choosing the right career path can be challenging. Our FAQs section is designed to provide you with clear and concise answers to common questions about our Dental Assisting and CNA programs. This section will help you make an informed decision and understand how our programs can fit into your busy schedule and career goals.

FAQs

Find answers to your questions about our training programs and enrollment process. We’re here to help you succeed!
What programs do you offer?
We offer Dental Assisting and Certified Nursing Assistant programs designed to prepare you for a rewarding career.
How long are the programs?
Our programs are short in length, focusing on core knowledge and techniques to get you job-ready quickly.
Are classes online or in-person?
We provide a hybrid model with online classes and hands-on clinical training to fit your schedule.
What are the enrollment requirements?
Enrollment requires a high school diploma or equivalent, and a passion for helping others in healthcare.
How can I apply?
You can apply directly through our website or contact us for more information on the application process.

Need More Help?

Contact us for any additional questions you may have.
